Signs You Need Commercial Water Heaters in South Jordan

A heater that is starting to struggle rarely hides it for long, and the clues are easy to spot once you know them. Keep an eye out for the following:

  • Loud sediment noise from equipment that runs all day
  • Rising gas or electric bills with no change in business volume
  • Visible corrosion or water pooling near the tank
  • Dish or hand sink temperatures drifting below what your inspector expects
  • Lukewarm water at the exact time you need the most
  • A heater old enough that a failure feels like a matter of time
  • Distant fixtures that take minutes to run warm
  • Unsteady delivery temperatures that suggest a failing mixing valve

Water Heater Care Tips From Our Technicians

Routine maintenance is almost always cheaper and easier than an early replacement. Here are our recommendations:

  • Inspect anode rods regularly, since heavy use consumes them faster
  • Record fixture temperatures regularly, especially before inspections
  • Have mixing valves checked so fixtures stay neither scalding nor lukewarm
  • Listen for a failing recirculation pump and have check valves inspected
  • Do not store supplies against the equipment or block air openings
  • Set a flushing schedule based on usage; busy tanks collect sediment fast
  • Budget for replacement as equipment nears the end of its expected life
