Prep sinks, pre-rinse sprayers, and dish cycles all draw from the same tank, a lunch rush can outrun a small heater quickly, and the dish machine needs a booster to reach its final rinse temperature. South Jordan Water Heater services, sizes, and repairs the tanks, tankless units, and booster heaters that keep both the kitchen and the restrooms supplied, and we schedule service around your operating hours.

We verify temperatures at the tank, the booster, and the fixtures and document every visit in written reports your inspector and landlord can review.

Equipment replacements and maintenance schedules are planned alongside owners, managers, and kitchen leads, so everything fits your operating schedule. When a job can't happen during service, we do it after close, so the kitchen opens the next day with hot water ready.

Code & Inspections

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Badly installed or neglected hot water equipment can create carbon monoxide hazards, scalding, Legionella risk in lukewarm storage, and expensive water damage when a tank fails. Those are real risks for any commercial property owner with employees, customers, and tenants in the building. Our commercial installations and inspections cover venting, combustion air, gas line sizing, relief valve discharge, expansion tanks, mixing valves, and code items like drain pans and seismic straps where required, and each visit ends with a written report listing risks, priorities, and recommended preventive steps.

The paperwork matters as much as the repair. We keep a service binder with detailed service logs, temperature readings, and relief valve test results, giving you proof of due diligence for landlords, insurers, and inspectors. Equipment that works hardest, in kitchens, laundries, and large mechanical rooms, is put on a more frequent visit schedule.
