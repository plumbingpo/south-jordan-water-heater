Getting More Years From Your Water Heater in West Jordan

A little routine care makes all the difference between a water heater that runs for years and one that floods the garage in year seven. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: an annual sediment flush, an anode inspection every few years, a yearly test of the T&P valve, a temperature setting around 120 degrees, and annual descaling for tankless units on hard water, so problems are found while they're still inexpensive.

Age matters too. Most tank water heaters last 8 to 12 years, tankless units often reach 20 with regular descaling, and heat pump models typically run 10 to 15. If your heater is past that window and showing warning signs like discolored hot water, rumbling during heating, moisture around the base, or hot water that runs out sooner than it used to, a planned replacement is almost always cheaper and far less stressful than an emergency one after the tank lets go.

The earlier you report a hot water problem, the cheaper it usually is. A sediment layer that's just starting to rumble needs only a flush, yet that same buildup, neglected through a few more years, can overheat the bottom of the tank and shorten the life of the tank, turning a simple visit into a replacement. Save our number on your phone now so help is one call away as soon as something sounds or looks wrong. Early intervention saves time, money, and ruined floors.

Common Water Heater Problems in West Jordan

In West Jordan, we work on every kind of water heater, and each one has a typical way of failing. Gas tank heaters tend to have trouble with thermocouples and flame sensors, sooty burners, and draft problems in the flue. Electric units burn out heating elements and thermostats, especially when sediment buries the lower element. Tankless units show error codes for scale in the heat exchanger, ignition faults, and flow sensor problems, most of which trace back to skipped descaling. Hybrid heat pump units need clean air filters and a clear condensate drain, and they struggle in cramped closets. We service all four types regularly and carry the parts to fix most problems on the first visit.
